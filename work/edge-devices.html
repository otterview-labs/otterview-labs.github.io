aibox

aibox is visual analysis software combining computer vision with vision-language models. CV detects objects and filters candidate events; a vision-language model reviews the scene against its rules for on-site video analysis, event assessment and result delivery.

  1. 01Ingest video
  2. 02Detect with CV
  3. 03Review with a VLM
  4. 04Return event results

CV finds candidate events

Ingest video, decode and sample frames, then run object detection. CV finds objects and candidate events and passes material that needs further assessment to a vision-language model.

Review the scene with a vision-language model

The model inspects candidate frames against scene rules to assess whether an event occurred. CV detection and vision-language review work within the same video processing workflow.

Bring models into on-site applications

The training platform prepares data, trains models and evaluates results. aibox handles deployment and runtime adaptation, coordinates video analysis and sends events and alerts to business systems.

Software and runtime environments

Our work focuses on deployment software, scene workflows and model adaptation. We validate results against the computing devices, models and video workload at each site. Scene and platform adaptations progress separately; availability depends on the version and its validation results.

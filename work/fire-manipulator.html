Fire panel manipulator

A robotic arm operates buttons on a fire alarm control panel. Prototype completed.

Mechanism

A pressing actuator on the robotic arm operates individual fire alarm control panel buttons.

Visual calibration

Locates the panel and its buttons and provides operating coordinates to the arm.

Installed equipment

Operates the buttons on installed fire alarm controllers without changing the controller itself.
